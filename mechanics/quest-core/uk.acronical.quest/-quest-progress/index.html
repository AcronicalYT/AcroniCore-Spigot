QuestProgress

open class QuestProgress

Tracks a specific player's ongoing progression through a quest.

This model handles the mutable state of quest objectives using thread-safe operations, ensuring accurate tracking even during highly concurrent asynchronous events (e.g., area-of-effect damage or rapid block breaking).
